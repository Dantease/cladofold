cladofold. 1.3.1 — preview
A softer close. A clearer open.

INSTALL
1. Open the disk image and drag cladofold. into Applications.
   With the ZIP download, unzip it and move the app into Applications.
   If you cannot write to /Applications, use your own ~/Applications folder.
2. Open the installed app. It installs its settings pane for your user account.
3. Choose “Check Mac compatibility…” to see whether your lid sensor is readable.
4. Choose “Open Screen Recording…” at the top of the app. Enable cladofold. in
   System Settings and complete Touch ID or your Mac password if asked.
   Accept “Quit & Reopen” if macOS asks. Return to cladofold. and wait for
   “Ready · follows your lid”; “Check again” reruns the screen-access check.
   A live lid sensor alone does not confirm the desktop animation is ready.
5. Try “Preview on my screen”, then close the lid partway and reopen it.
   The miniature preview works without screen access; the desktop effect needs it.
6. Choose “Open in System Settings” to adjust the animation and its on/off switch.

THIS IS AN UNNOTARIZED PREVIEW
This preview has a local ad-hoc signature, not a Developer ID signature or
Apple notarization. macOS may block it on first launch. If you trust the person
who provided it, try opening it, then use System Settings > Privacy & Security
> Open Anyway for this app. Managed Macs may not permit this.
Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/102445
A normal notarized release still requires the developer's Apple Developer ID.

REQUIREMENTS
• macOS Sonoma 14.0 or later, with Metal graphics.
• Universal app: native Apple silicon and Intel binaries are both included.
• A built-in display and a readable lid-angle sensor for automatic folding.
• Screen Recording permission for the desktop effect. No Accessibility,
  Input Monitoring, camera, microphone, or administrator access is required
  by the app. Installing in the system Applications folder may need admin access.

EXPECTED SENSOR SUPPORT — verify with the app's compatibility check
• MacBook Pro: 14-inch and 16-inch models, including the 2019 16-inch Intel model.
• MacBook Air: M2 and later, both 13-inch and 15-inch models.
• M1 MacBook Air, 13-inch MacBook Pro (including M1/M2 Touch Bar), most older
  Intel MacBooks, and the 12-inch MacBook do not have the required angle sensor.
  They can use the previews, but cannot follow the lid continuously.
These are research-based expectations, not a claim that every model is tested.
This build was tested on an Apple M5 Max MacBook Pro; Intel and older macOS
versions need real-device testing. There is no model whitelist: any Mac exposing
Apple's compatible sensor can work, including future models.

USE
Start with your lid at a comfortable angle. Closing begins the effect from there.
“Black at” controls the full-darkness endpoint (30° above fully closed by default).
Reopen to your starting angle
to clear. Blur stays at a held angle by default; turn off “Keep blur until I open
the lid back up” to clear after half a second of stillness. Turn off “Start when
I begin closing” to choose manual angle thresholds.
“Duo fold animation” adds the dark borders, perspective, blur and fade to black.
“Dark border”, “Maximum blur”, “Darkening” and “Smoothing” adjust the effect.
The menu-bar cf. button also provides settings, preview and Quit.
Control–Option–Command–B disables the effect immediately.
The desktop preview runs for six seconds and clears automatically.

PRIVACY AND BEHAVIOR
The app captures one temporary desktop frame per fold. The image freezes during
that animation while your applications keep running underneath. Frames remain
in memory on your Mac, are discarded when the effect clears, and are never
saved or transmitted. The app contains no analytics or automatic updater.
The effect clears on sleep, lock, display changes, sensor loss or disable.
macOS owns the lock screen and sleep behavior; an animation cannot run while
asleep or cover the lock screen. External displays are not affected.

TROUBLESHOOTING
An orange cf. menu-bar mark means the animation is not ready. The setup card
at the top of the app explains whether it needs screen access, a working sensor,
an enabled effect, or an awake built-in display. Automatic capture checks require
macOS to allow access and stop after a failed check, even across app restarts.
A newly granted permission or your explicit “Check again” action permits a retry.
A successful screen capture is required before the app reports verified access.
Repeatedly dismissing or approving a prompt does not repair an outdated entry.
If an updated preview cannot capture despite permission being enabled, remove
only its old entry in Screen & System Audio Recording, add the installed
cladofold. app again, enable it, and quit/reopen when prompted.
Quit and reopen System Settings if its pane was already open during an update.
The app's name ends in a period. With extensions shown, its filename is
“cladofold..app”; the two periods are intentional.

REMOVE
Turn off “Launch at login”, quit the app, and move it to Trash.
Also move ~/Library/PreferencePanes/cladofold..prefPane to Trash.
You can remove its Screen Recording entry in Privacy & Security.

SHARE
Send the complete DMG or ZIP, including these instructions, to your recipient.
They do not need Xcode, Homebrew, or any developer tools.
